Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers the Leaps in AI website and "Leaps in AI Poster", a private publishing tool that Leaps in AI uses to post its own videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

Who can use the tool

Leaps in AI Poster is not offered to the public. Only the owner of the Leaps in AI channels signs in to it. It does not let anyone else sign in, and it does not access other people's accounts.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): uploading and publishing Leaps in AI's own videos, reading the list of its own uploads to avoid duplicates, and reading its own channel and video statistics (views, watch time, likes, shares, subscribers).
  • Instagram (via the Meta Graph API): publishing Leaps in AI's own reels and reading their insights.

The tool does not collect, store or process personal data about viewers or any other users.

How data is stored and shared

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only in systems controlled by the Leaps in AI owner and are used only to publish our videos and measure how they perform. They are never sold or shared with anyone.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

This website

This site has no sign-in, forms, cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting data

Revoking the app's access in Google or Meta settings stops all access immediately. Locally stored tokens and statistics are deleted when the tool is retired.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @leapsinai on Instagram.
